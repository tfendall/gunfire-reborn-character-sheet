Gunfire Character Sheet 0.13.37 (experimental)

Damage totals now follow the game's scoreboard, with any remaining difference
shown explicitly as Unattributed damage. Its expanded explanation reads:
"Can include damage to immortal or invincible enemies, or slight rounding errors"

POLE MONARCH Orb damage uses the game's seasonal breakdown instead of a
reproduced formula. Both orb definitions are recognized, including when an orb
is acquired after a zero-total reset. Missing per-hit attribution remains
visible; a residual is not automatically assigned to the orb or to rounding.

Effective bonuses resolved through the game's logic populate the existing
contributor rows and cumulative values. Unresolved contributions remain unknown.

Research diagnostics are disabled by default. The observers remain available
through EnableResearch; see DEBUGGING.txt. Normal operation keeps authoritative
totals and live effective bonuses without research trace output. Optional trace
logging has per-session and directory storage limits.

Known limitation: observed Immortal reaction packets do not provide numeric
per-hit damage credit. The scoreboard total includes that credit, but its source
can remain Unattributed.

The preceding 0.13.36 gameplay session loaded successfully with research off and
no logged errors or exceptions. This release updates the explanation and release
documentation. Automated build, regression, installer and package checks are
performed before publication. Frame-time overhead has not been benchmarked.
