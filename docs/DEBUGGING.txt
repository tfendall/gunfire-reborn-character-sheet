Gunfire Character Sheet 0.13.37: optional research diagnostics

Default release operation
Research is disabled. No broad combat, outgoing hit, death, tooltip, or property
research hooks are installed. No JSONL trace writer or callback journal starts.
The original damage capture and authoritative scoreboard observer remain active.
UseGameDamageTotals and ShowEffectiveBonuses in [Stats] default to true. They do
not require research. UseGameDamageTotals also enables automatic scoreboard
sampling, limited to one outstanding request and at most one request per second.
The former [Diagnostics] AutomaticScoreboardSampling setting is no longer used.

Turn observers back on
Close the game. Edit BepInEx/config/local.gunfire.statsdiagnostic.cfg:
[Diagnostics]
EnableResearch = true
CaptureScoreboardDamage = true
CaptureCombatEvidence = true
Restart. Those two switches enable scoreboard reconciliation/raw packet/death
analysis and the broad combat bundle (including outgoing hit observers).
Additional optional switches, normally false:
CaptureStatusDetails
CaptureTooltipCalculations
CaptureEffectiveProperties
CaptureStateChanges
CaptureGameplayCalculations
RefreshEffectCalculations
F10's selected experimental observer also requires EnableResearch.
To stop research, set EnableResearch = false and restart. Other switches may be
left in place for future debugging. Upgrades retain settings, but old observer
flags alone do not activate the new master switch.

Storage
New JSONL files are under BepInEx/StatsDiagnostic/Research. Rotation happens at
4 MiB or 10,000 records. Limits: 64 MiB per session and 256 MiB of trace files
in this directory at startup plus this session. These limits assume a single
game process. Logging stops at capacity; F8 trace health reports the limit.
Archive needed traces and move/remove old research traces while the game is
closed before recording more. Prior traces and callback journals are preserved;
this build does not delete old investigation evidence automatically. Ordinary
BepInEx startup/errors remain in its normal log even when research is off.

Reporting
Include the mod version, relevant trace session and all its -part files when
research was enabled. Note queue drops/truncation or a reached storage limit;
incomplete logs cannot establish that an event never occurred.
